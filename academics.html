Curriculum

Learning for today, prepared for tomorrow

Our curriculum combines strong academic foundations with practical skills, character, and confidence for a changing world.

Our approach

A rich and diverse learning experience

The school offers a comprehensive and diversified curriculum aligned with Uganda’s New Lower Secondary Curriculum. This learner-centered approach is designed to equip students with essential knowledge, practical skills, and competencies for the modern world.

Through this broad subject offering, the school nurtures well-rounded learners with critical thinking skills, creativity, and practical competence, preparing them for higher education, innovation, and future careers in a rapidly evolving global society.

What learners develop

  • KnowledgeStrong foundations for further study
  • Practical skillsConfidence to apply learning in real life
  • CharacterValues that shape responsible citizens
  • CreativityCuriosity, innovation, and problem-solving

Our learners build strong foundations in class and then put their knowledge into practice.

Lower Secondary School

Senior 1 to Senior 4 curriculum

In the new lower secondary curriculum from Senior 1 to 4, learners are expected to take the following subjects. At Matugga Hills Secondary School, learners study a set of core subjects and choose from a range of elective subjects that allow them to follow their own interests and specialize in specific areas.

Core subjects

Compulsory subjects include:

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • History and Political Education
  • Geography
  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Physics
  • Literature in English
  • Entrepreneurship
  • ICT

Languages, sciences, and applied subjects

  • Agriculture
  • Physical Education
  • Christian Religious Education
  • Islamic Religious Studies
  • Kiswahili
  • Luganda
